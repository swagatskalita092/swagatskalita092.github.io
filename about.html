About

A bit more context than a résumé has room for.

I'm an M.Sc. Informatik student at the University of Passau, originally from Guwahati, India, currently working toward software engineering and data engineering roles across Germany and the EU.

What I actually care about in a project is whether a claim I make about it is true — not whether it sounds impressive. That's the thread through everything on the Work page: a named invariant, a real adversarial test against it, and an honest writeup of what broke along the way. I wrote that discipline down as a public methodology, the Fullproof Project Toolkit, so it's not just something I say about my own work.

Outside of building things: I play competitive tennis (currently on TC Rot-Weiss Passau's men's team in the BTV league) and I'm learning German (currently working toward B1).
